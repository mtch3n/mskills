# Design

Tokens are in `site/app.css` (`:root`). Use them. Do not write raw colors in a page.

## Components

- `.page`: the page container.
- `.card`: a bordered box. Plans, the invoice table and forms use it.
- `.button`, `.button.outline`, `.icon-button`, `.input`: controls.
- `.menu`: the row action menu of a table.

## Rules from review

- Show the full invoice number. People read it to the bank on the phone.
- Do not change the header.
