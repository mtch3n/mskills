# Design

Tokens are in `site/app.css` (`:root`). Use them. Do not write raw colors in a page.

## Components

- `.page`: the page container. Every page uses it.
- `.chip`: a role or status label. Every chip looks the same on every page.
- `.invite`: the invite link with its Copy button.
- `.table`, `.input`, `.button`: tables and form controls.

## Rules from review

- Do not shorten or hide the invite link. People copy it from the box.
- Do not change the header.
