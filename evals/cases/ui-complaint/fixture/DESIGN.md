# Design

Tokens are in `site/app.css` (`:root`). Use them. Do not write raw colors or sizes in a page.

## Components

- `.page`: the page container. Every page uses it.
- `.table`: every data table.
- `.card`: a surface for one group of settings.
- `.input`, `.button`: form controls. They are 36px tall.

## Rules from review

- Do not add a helper sentence under a heading.
- Do not change the header or the navigation.
